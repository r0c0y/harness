# Option comparison

1. Define the decision and the non-negotiable constraints.
2. Compare at least two viable options on correctness, compatibility, effort, operational risk, and reversibility.
3. Distinguish evidence from estimates; do not give false precision to scores.
4. Recommend the smallest option that meets the stated goal and explain the main tradeoff.
5. Ask the user only if the final choice depends on a preference or permission they have not supplied.
